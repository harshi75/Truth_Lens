Five ways a claim can be reported.

Supported

Reliable evidence substantially supports the claim.

Contradicted

Reliable evidence directly conflicts with the claim.

Mixed

Some parts are supported while others are incomplete or contradicted.

Outdated

The factual situation changed after the claim was accurate.

Insufficient

Not enough reliable evidence to determine the claim.
